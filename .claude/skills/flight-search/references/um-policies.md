## Key Rules by Age

### Age 13 (Rika as of 2026)
- **BLOCKED**: British Airways (minimum solo age 14, no exceptions)
- **BLOCKED on connections**: JAL (other airline connections), ANA (other airline connections), Turkish Airlines (all connections)
- **SAFE for connections**: Emirates (DXB hub, USD50/sector), Qatar Airways (DOH hub, free), Thai Airways (<6h same airport)
- **DIRECT flights safe**: JAL, ANA, Emirates, Qatar, Thai, Singapore, Finnair, Cathay Pacific

### Age 17 (Eita as of 2026)
- Treated as adult or near-adult by virtually all airlines
- **ONLY EXCLUSION**: Turkish Airlines connections (companion required regardless of age)
- All other airlines: no UM service required, no restrictions

## Notes
- Policies change. Always verify with the airline before booking, especially for connections.
- Ages are as of 2026. Update this file as children age up.
- Korean Air UM policy was not confirmed during initial research. Verify before booking ICN connections for minors under 15.
